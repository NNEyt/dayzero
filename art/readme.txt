The cast (graphic-novel artwork, used in the story text and the story track):
- Kit: the kid in the yellow raincoat. Runs every message (and is the 👟 Runner kid role).
- Marta: the gran in the green scarf. Knows everyone, especially in Hillside (🏠 Local).
- Sam: the dad with the handheld radio. Repeats every rumour he hears; can become an ally or a rival.
- Ines: orange beanie. Runs the hall's stores and drives the van (📦 Quartermaster).
- Theo: headphones. Runs the hall's radio desk (📻 Signal).
- Pavel: the night-shift nurse (✚ Medic).

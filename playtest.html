🕯️ Day Zero playtest: host checklist

Session date:

Goal: check that the game creates table talk, ownership, tension and “again!”, not just polish. Observe quietly: don't explain beyond what the game says, and note every “what do we do now?”.

Before they arrive

  • Open the game on the TV/laptop. Full screen (F11). Sound on (🔊 top right).
  • Use the same seed for every group: open …/?seed=K7Q2 (or type K7Q2 in ⚙️ Game options → Town seed).
  • Start fresh with ▶ Play, not “Continue saved game”.
  • Phones charged and on the same internet as the TV (controller mode only).
  • Recording consent from everyone (audio is enough). Print the cards; pens ready.

Settings for this session

  • Length: Quick game · 5 Days (≈40 min). For the variety test, play two Quick games back to back (new seed for the second).
  • Screens: TV + phone controllers if the Wi-Fi is good; otherwise TV only.
  • Difficulty: Normal. Personal goals: on. Secret votes: on.
  • Insider: off for a first family game; on only with 3+ players aged 12+.
  • Tick 🧒 next to younger players (7–11). With 5+ players they get Kit or Bea.

During play

  • Note the clock time when Play is pressed and at the first decision (target: under 2 min).
  • Hand the observer sheet to a second adult if you're playing.
  • At the end, let them read the Chronicle. Note if anyone shares or photographs it.

📱 If phones won't connect

  • Every device needs internet, even on the same Wi-Fi. Try one phone on mobile data.
  • Strict Wi-Fi (school, office, hotel) can block the link. Use a phone hotspot for the TV, or switch mode.
  • A phone that slept or reloaded: reopen the same QR link; it rejoins and gets its screen back.
  • One phone stuck? Use its “on this screen” button on the TV. Nobody holds up the game.
  • Lobby says the link failed: press Play without phones.

📺 Falling back to TV mode

  • Mid-game: carry on using the TV buttons; phones are optional.
  • Before starting: ⚙️ Game options → Screens → TV only, or TV + phone briefings (QR codes, no connection needed).
  • Keep the same seed so results stay comparable.

After the game (5 min)

  • Hand out the feedback cards before any discussion.
  • Ask: Which decision did you argue about most? Did anyone feel left out or told what to do? What would you tell a friend this game is about?
  • Note if anyone asks “Can we play again?” unprompted.
  • Next day: text everyone “What's one moment you remember?” (target ≥75% can retell one).
